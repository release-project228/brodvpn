brodvpn

VPN · privacy · iOS · Android · Windows

brodvpn is a privacy-focused VPN service with apps for iOS, Android and Windows. It encrypts the connection between your device and the internet so you can use public Wi-Fi safely, keep your traffic away from prying eyes and reach sites that are restricted in your region.

The service is built around a simple idea: a VPN should be easy to start and easy to trust. You connect with one tap, pick a server location, and the app handles the rest. There are no confusing settings to dig through before you are protected and no bandwidth caps on the standard plan.

  • iOS and Android apps for phone and tablet
  • Windows app for desktop
  • One subscription across your devices

Whether you travel often, work from cafés with public Wi-Fi or simply want more privacy at home, brodvpn keeps your connection protected without slowing you down.

The full service runs at https://brodvpn.com.
